Privacy

Privacy notice

This page explains what we do with the information you send us. It's a pre-launch draft and will be finalised with our registered company details before the site goes live.

Who we are

Brick & Pane is the data controller for information collected through this website. Contact: hello@brickandpane.co.uk. Our registered company name, number and address will be added here before launch.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in the enquiry form or by email: your name, email, phone (optional), company, project location and details, and any files you upload.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the website securely.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare reviews and quotations, and deliver projects you ask us to carry out. The lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become projects: up to 24 months, then deleted. Project records: as long as needed for guarantees and legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers who help us run the business (for example, email and form hosting), under contract. We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. The enquiry form may keep an unsent draft in your own browser so you don't lose it. Nothing is sent to us until you submit.

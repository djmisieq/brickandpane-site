What we take responsibility for

Glazing sits on the critical path. It closes the building, and late or wrong frames hold up every trade after them.

Cost

Itemised, fixed quotation

Priced line by line against your schedule, with clear exclusions. Early budgets are given as £/m² and per opening for appraisals.

Programme

Dates in writing

Lead time runs from signed drawings. Deliveries are phased to suit the build sequence, and you hear about any slip from us first.

Quality

Approval and inspection

Approved shop drawings before production. Each delivery is checked against the signed drawings.

Visibility

Live project portal

Every package has a portal page: stage, owner, promised dates, documents and approvals, visible to your whole team without a status call.

See the portal

Compliance

Documentation built in

Declarations of performance, PAS 24 evidence, U-value certificates and an as-built register, ready for building control and your records.

From tender to handover

We plan London logistics in from the start: loading bays, parking suspensions and lifting plans.

  1. Budget

    Schedule or drawings in. £/m² and per-opening budget out, with value-engineering options listed separately.

    → 5 working days

  2. Tender

    Itemised fixed quotation, programme and method statement.

    → fixed price

  3. Design

    Survey, shop drawings and interface details, signed off by your design team.

    → signed drawings

  4. Manufacture

    Made to the signed drawings, with a mock-up approved first and progress reported weekly.

    → phased deliveries

  5. Install & hand over

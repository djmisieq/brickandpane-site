Which door is right for me?

All three work beautifully in a London extension. Ask yourself one question: how will you use it most of the year?

Common worries

Will it be draughty, leak, or break the rules?

Will big glass doors be cold or draughty?

Not if they're made and fitted well. Modern aluminium doors have a thermal break inside the frame and double or triple glazing. Lift & slide doors also press down onto rubber seals when closed, so they're especially draught-proof. We tell you the exact insulation figure for your doors before you order.

Will rain come in at the bottom?

A completely level floor from kitchen to garden needs a drain outside the door and the right floor build-up, which your builder needs to know about early. We give them the details. On exposed spots, we may suggest a tiny step (15 mm or less) instead. It's just as easy to walk over and even safer against heavy rain.

Do I need planning permission?

Many rear extensions don't, because they count as “permitted development”. Conservation areas and listed buildings have stricter rules, though. Check your address on the Planning Portal or ask your council. If you need drawings for an application, we can provide elevations of your doors.

What about Building Regulations? I don't have an architect.

That's fine. Your builder deals with building control for the extension. For the doors, the main points are insulation, safety glass and security, and we provide the figures and certificates for all three. If the extension has a lot of glass (more than about a quarter of its floor area), an energy calculation called SAP is needed. It's a routine step done by an energy assessor, not a ban, and we give them the numbers they need.

Are they secure?

We specify to the PAS 24 security standard, with multi-point locks and toughened or laminated safety glass.

How do the glass panels get into my house?

Big panels are heavy, so we plan the route from the street at the survey. Sometimes it's through the house, and occasionally it needs a small crane, which may need a council permit. We handle that and tell you in advance.

You're a new company. Why should I trust you?

That's a fair question. You can see a full-size corner of your actual door before you order. You pay in stages, never all up front. Our own team does the fitting, and everything is in writing. Read about our founding projects, which open to homeowners as well as architects.

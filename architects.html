What we do for architects

Each service has a clear output.

01 · Any stage

Drawing & schedule review

Upload plans, elevations or a window schedule (PDF, DWG or XLSX). You get comments on panel sizes and weights, Part L, K, M, O and Q points, structural and threshold interfaces. The price follows as an individual quote.

Send for review

02 · Stage 3–4

Project details

Head, sill, jamb and threshold details drawn to your wall build-up, plus the deflection limit and fixing loads for your engineer. Issued as DWG, ready to drop into your set.

03 · 45 minutes

CPD for your practice

Lunchtime sessions in your studio or online: Designing large sliding doors in London extensions and Glazing and the 2021 Building Regulations (L, O, K, Q). Accreditation is in progress.

What each Approved Document asks of the glazing.

A quick reference for England. We supply the data for each item on your project. Always check the current edition and any local requirements.

PartWhat it means for doors & windowsWhat we provide
L — Conservation of fuel and powerLimiting U-values: 1.4 W/m²K for new or replacement units in existing homes, 1.6 in new dwellings. For extensions, glazing over 25% of the floor area (plus openings removed) means using SAP.Certified Uw for your actual sizes, plus Ug, g-value and frame data for SAP.
O — OverheatingNew dwellings: limit solar gain and provide ventilation. Large south or west glazing is the usual risk.g-values for solar control glass, and opening areas and restrictor data.
K — Protection from falling, impactSafety glazing in critical locations, including doors and side panels near floor level. Guarding where sills are low.Toughened or laminated glass classification for every pane.
M — AccessAccessible thresholds in new dwellings: a total upstand of no more than 15 mm.Flush and ≤15 mm threshold details.
Q — SecurityDoors and easily reachable windows in new dwellings to be tested to PAS 24 (or equivalent).PAS 24 test evidence for the system and configuration.
F — VentilationBackground ventilation for habitable rooms. Sliders rarely take trickle vents, so plan it elsewhere.Equivalent-area data for vents in windows.

No substitutions without your sign-off

Value engineering shouldn't happen behind your back. Our change control is simple:

  1. No substitutions without sign-off

    Profiles, glass, hardware and finishes are installed exactly as specified.

  2. Every change in writing

    Any change request shows its effect on U-value, security, weather class and certification before anything is ordered.

  3. A record you can keep

    At handover you get a register of the as-built specification and declarations of performance, ready for your project records.

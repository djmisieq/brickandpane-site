About

Openings in brick, done properly.

Brick & Pane makes aluminium lift & slide doors, sliding doors and windows to order for London homes and residential schemes. We started the company because specifying big glass is still harder than it should be. The data is hidden, the details don't belong to anyone, and the product that arrives isn't always the one that was drawn.

How we work

Five rules

  1. Publish the data

    Performance figures go on the website, and certified values go on your drawings.

  2. Own the interface

    We draw the head, sill, jamb and threshold with you, not after you.

  3. Install what was specified

    No substitutions without written sign-off.

  4. Fit with our own team

    Our people survey, deliver and install.

  5. Say it straight

    Honest lead times and honest answers, including “that won't work”.

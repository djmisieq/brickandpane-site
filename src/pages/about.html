About

A London windows and doors company: who we are and how we work.

Brick & Pane makes windows, doors, large sliding doors and glazed facades to order, in aluminium, timber and PVC, for London homes and residential schemes. We started the company because specifying glazing is still harder than it should be. The data is hidden, the details don't belong to anyone, and the product that arrives isn't always the one that was drawn.

Five rules

  1. Publish the data

    Performance figures go on the website, and certified values go on your drawings.

  2. Own the interface

    We draw the head, sill, jamb and threshold with you, not after you.

  3. Install what was specified

    No substitutions without written sign-off.

  4. Survey, deliver, fit

    We survey, deliver and install, with one project manager throughout.

  5. Say it straight

    Honest lead times and honest answers, including “that won't work”.

Where your doors are made

Privacy notice

This page explains what we do with the information you send us.

Who we are

The data controller for the information described on this page is Christopher Rozek, a sole trader trading as Brick and Pane, 30 Pettman Close, Herne Bay, Kent, CT6 5TJ, United Kingdom. Contact: contact@brickandpane.co.uk, +44 7575 626 775.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in the enquiry form or by email: your name, email, phone (optional), company, project location and details, and any files you upload.
  • If you create a project portal account: your name, email address, account type and, if you add them, company and phone, plus what you and we add to your projects (documents, messages, approvals and stage updates). We never see your password.
  • If you sign in with Google or Microsoft: the basic profile that service shares with us, which is your name, email address and, where provided, a profile picture link. We don't get access to anything else in your Google or Microsoft account.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the website securely.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare reviews and quotations, and deliver projects you ask us to carry out. If you have a portal account, also to sign you in, show you your projects and email you when something in them changes. You can turn these emails off in your portal account settings. The lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Business contacts we approach

We may email architects, developers and building contractors to introduce our services. For this we use work contact details that businesses publish themselves, for example on their own websites or in company and professional directories: name, job title, company, and work email address or phone number.

The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in offering our services to businesses that design, specify or buy windows and doors. We write only to work addresses, about work that fits your business, and every email tells you how to opt out. Reply "unsubscribe" or email us and we stop. We then keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list, so we don't write to you again.

If you don't reply, we delete your details within 12 months of our last email. If you reply, we handle your details like any other enquiry.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become projects: up to 24 months, then deleted. Project records: as long as needed for guarantees and legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers who help us run the business, under contract. We never sell your data. They are:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage and sign-in. Your enquiries, portal account and project data are stored in its London (UK) region.
  • Resend: sends our emails, such as replies to enquiries, sign-in links and portal notifications.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • Google and Microsoft: only if you choose to sign in with them.

Resend and GitHub are based in the United States. The emails we send you, and the technical data your browser sends when it loads this site (such as your IP address), may therefore be processed in the US, under the safeguards these providers offer for transfers from the UK.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can stop marketing emails at any time. Email us at the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. The enquiry form may keep an unsent draft in your own browser so you don't lose it, and the list of openings you add on the product pages is kept there too until you send it. Nothing is sent to us until you submit. If you sign in to the project portal, your browser keeps a sign-in token so you don't have to sign in every time. Signing out removes it.

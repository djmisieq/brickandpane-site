Why us

The problems we set out to remove.

Ask architects and contractors what goes wrong with glazing packages and you hear the same four complaints. We built the company around fixing them.

“The data was ‘on request’”

Published, not promised

U-values, panel limits, security and weather classes are on each system page, with the certified figure for your sizes at Stage 4.

“They swapped the system”

No silent substitutions

What you specify is what we install. Any change comes to you in writing with its effect on performance first. That's change control you can put in the golden thread.

“Nobody owned the detail”

One team, drawing to handover

Our project manager owns the interface details, the survey, the delivery plan and the installation. We don't subcontract the fitting.

“The lead time moved”

A programme you can build to

Lead times run from signed drawings and are agreed in writing. If our programme slips, you hear it from us first, with a new date.

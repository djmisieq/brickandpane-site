Aluminium, timber or PVC? We sell all three, so we can tell you honestly.

Most suppliers sell one material and say it suits everything. Each one has a job it does best, and in London the building, the planning officer and the budget usually decide between them.

Aluminium

Slimmest frames, biggest glass

Strong enough for heavy panels and narrow sightlines. Any RAL colour, very low maintenance, fully recyclable. Usually the answer for large sliders, facades and steel-look windows.

Timber

The right look for period houses

Sliding sashes and flush casements that conservation officers expect. Warm to the touch, renewable when FSC-certified, and repairable for decades with a recoat every few years.

PVC

Best value per opening

Good thermal performance for the money and almost no upkeep. A sensible choice for rentals, side elevations and windows that don’t need slim frames, outside conservation areas.

Also

Timber-aluminium

Timber inside, an aluminium shell outside. The warmth of wood in the room with weather-proof, low-maintenance faces outside, at a higher price.

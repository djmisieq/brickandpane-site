Systems

One material, done properly.

Every system is thermally broken aluminium with the same finishes and hardware family. Mix lift & slide at the back, French doors at the side and tilt-turn windows upstairs, and the whole house still matches.

Flagship · full data

Lift & slide doors

Panels to 400 kg · to 3.3 × 3.2 m · interlock from c. 50 mm Indicative

The panel lifts onto rollers to slide, then drops onto compressed gaskets to seal. Best for wide garden elevations, big glass and exposed sites.

  • 2, 3, 4 and 6-panel layouts, a post-free corner and a pocket
  • Flush or ≤15 mm threshold
  • Sizing tool, performance table and spec pack

Slim sliding doors

Panels to 250 kg · interlock from c. 35 mm · flush threshold Indicative

Inline sliders with very slim interlocks for contemporary elevations, where the panel sizes and weights suit standard hardware. Often the better choice where budget matters and the panels are moderate.

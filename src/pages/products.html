Frames

Branded, tested systems

Thermally broken aluminium, engineered timber and multi-chamber PVC, from system houses that publish their test results.

Finish

Matched across materials

RAL powder coat on aluminium, factory-applied stain or paint on timber, foils or colour-through on PVC, chosen to match from a single sample board.

Glass

Double or triple, safety-rated

Low-e, warm edge and argon. Toughened or laminated where Part K requires it. Solar control and acoustic options.

Compliance

Data for Parts B, F, K, L, M, O and Q

Certified U-values, PAS 24 security, escape windows, trickle vents, accessible thresholds and g-values for overheating checks.

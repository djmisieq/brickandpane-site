Founding projects

Founding projects: what you can check before you choose us.

We're a new company, so there's no portfolio here yet, and we won't fill this page with stock images. We're taking on a small number of founding projects.

Apply as a founding project

Designed to take the risk out of choosing someone new.

Every point below is something you can verify before you sign, not a promise about our track record.

Before you order

System data and test references

System test reports for thermal, weather and security performance, published as each system is certified.

Before you order

Survey and measurement

We survey the existing openings and measure on site before anything goes into production, so the drawings match the building.

Money

Payments staged against delivery

Deposit, then payments tied to delivery and completion, never the full value up front. A guarantee from a UK company.

In return

  • Permission to photograph the finished project professionally.
  • A short, honest case study: the brief, the constraints, the details we used and what we'd improve.
  • A quote from you or your architect, attributed or anonymous, your choice.

Each case study will show real data: the borough, building type, system, U-value, programme and the problem we solved.

The projects we're looking for

Rear & side-return extensions

Victorian and Edwardian terraces with a wide lift & slide or corner opening to the garden.

Contemporary new builds

Single houses and mews with large glazing and flush thresholds.

Small residential schemes

Developments of 4–30 units where a package of doors and windows needs one accountable supplier.

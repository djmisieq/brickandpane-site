Founding projects

No borrowed photos. Proof you can check instead.

We're a new company, so there's no portfolio here yet, and we won't fill this page with stock images. We're taking on a small number of founding projects. They get more of our attention, more checks and stronger terms than any client will later.

Apply as a founding project
What founding clients get

Designed to take the risk out of choosing someone new.

Every point below is something you can verify before you sign, not a promise about our track record.

Before you order

Certified data and test references

System test reports for thermal, weather and security performance, and the certified Uw for your actual sizes.

Before you order

Full-size corner sample

A corner of your actual door, in your finish and glass, brought to your studio, site or home to inspect and keep.

During the project

Director-led, our own installers

A company director runs each founding project from survey to handover. Installation is never subcontracted.

Money

Payments staged against delivery

Deposit, then payments tied to delivery and completion, never the full value up front. A guarantee from a UK company.

What we ask

In return

  • Permission to photograph the finished project professionally. You get the photographs too.
  • A short, honest case study: the brief, the constraints, the details we used and what we'd improve.
  • A quote from you or your architect, attributed or anonymous, your choice.

Each case study will show real data: the borough, building type, system, U-value, programme and the problem we solved.

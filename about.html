About

Brick for the city. Pane for the light.

A new London company specifying and installing precision-made windows, doors and facades to London & the Home Counties.

Our approach

London's buildings are defined by brick — and by the openings set within it. We believe those openings deserve engineering of the highest quality and installation done with patience and care.

Our UK team specifies, supplies and installs precision-made timber, aluminium and uPVC systems, with UK-based service and aftercare that allow us to meet the needs of both architects and homeowners.

What we value

  • Precision in specification and installation
  • Calm, tidy, well-communicated installation
  • Honest lead times and pricing
  • Respect for London's architecture, old and new
